Everything your plants need

Designed from the ground up to offer rich plant care functionality without compromising on speed or privacy.

Plant & Room Management

Group your houseplants by room or location. Maintain detailed profiles for each plant with customized lighting and watering requirements.

💧

Smart Care Schedules

Set flexible reminders for watering, fertilizing, misting, pruning, or repotting. Keep a complete log of past care activities.

🌱

Propagation Tracker

Monitor your cuttings and saplings through every root milestone. Track propagation medium, start date, and success rate.

📸

Visual Diary & Photo Log

Capture photos directly in the app to document growth progress or identify problems over time. Saved securely in local app storage.

🛡️

100% On-Device Privacy

No registration, no accounts, and no background tracking. Your plant collection, photos, and notes never leave your phone.

🔒 Privacy First / Prywatność Przede Wszystkim

Your data belongs to you. Period.

Mandragora is engineered as an offline-first application. All data—including plant records, care history, photos, and diary entries—is saved in an on-device database and local app storage. We do not maintain user servers or analytics trackers.

✓ No user accounts or sign-in
✓ No telemetry or advertising SDKs
✓ Camera access only for local photos
✓ Complete control: uninstalling deletes all data
